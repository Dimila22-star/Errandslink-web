How it works

1

Send a photo

Upload a picture of the item and tell us roughly what you can pay.

2

Sellers reply

Sellers in the market see your request and say if they have it and how much.

3

We bargain

We chat with the sellers and push the price down as far as we can.

4

We deliver

We buy it, check that it's good quality, and send it to you.

About us

Shopping in a busy market eats up your whole day, and you never really know if you got a fair price. ErrandLink started from that problem.

We charge a small fee for the errand. If you'd rather see the goods yourself, we can also take you to the seller for a little extra.
